# Framed arm — verbatim advertisement text

Transcribed from `data.csv` in `../ad_assets_export_with_images.zip` (Google
Ads Editor export, 15 August 2026); the export is authoritative if they ever
disagree. The same
six ad groups appear identically in all ten framed campaigns
(`MA2026_framed_01` … `MA2026_framed_10`). Status at export: campaigns
*Pending* (scheduled start), ad groups and ads *Enabled*, approval status
*Approved* for every ad. No video assets and no custom App URL (installs go
to the default Play Store listing). Language: Spanish.

The arm crosses the **two regulatory-focus orientations** (prevention /
promotion) with **three motivational themes** (personal experience, community
action, scientific contribution), giving six ad groups. Google's optimiser
allocates impressions among them within each campaign; the
prevention-versus-promotion contrast is exploratory only (see the
pre-registration's *Other planned analysis*).

Text is verbatim, including typography and capitalization as configured in
the account (e.g. `notíficalo`, and the lower-case initial in one
description); do not "correct" it — the record must show exactly what ran.
English glosses in italics are unofficial translations for readers of this
repository; they are not part of the stimulus. Headlines are limited by
Google to 30 characters, descriptions to 90.

Image assets: every framed ad group carries exactly 20 images (Google's
maximum) — 17 shared with the neutral arm, plus three crops of the ad
group's orientation image: `Aedes-albopictus-macro-2.jpg` (mosquito on skin
— prevention) in the three Prevention ad groups, `prommosq.jpg` (mosquito on
leaf — promotion) in the three Promotion ad groups. The authoritative
per-ad-group mapping is `data.csv` inside
`../ad_assets_export_with_images.zip`; see `../README.md` for the image map.
