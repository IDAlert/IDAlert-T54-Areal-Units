# Neutral arm — verbatim advertisement text

Transcribed from `data.csv` in `../ad_assets_export_with_images.zip` (Google
Ads Editor export, 15 August 2026); the export is authoritative if they ever
disagree. The same
single ad group (`Ad group 1`) appears identically in all ten neutral
campaigns (`MA2026_neutral_01` … `MA2026_neutral_10`). Status at export:
campaigns *Pending* (scheduled start), ad group and ad *Enabled*, approval
status *Approved*. No video assets and no custom App URL. Language: Spanish.

The copy describes the application's function — report mosquitoes and
standing water, see the map — without invoking either regulatory-focus
orientation: no threat, risk or protection language (prevention), and no
achievement, discovery or community-building language (promotion). English
glosses in italics are unofficial translations, not part of the stimulus.
Headlines are limited by Google to 30 characters, descriptions to 90.

Image assets: the neutral ad group carries exactly 20 images (Google's
maximum) — 17 shared with the framed arm, plus three crops of the
neutral-only image (`Aedes_albopictus_on_the_wall_-_2.jpg`, mosquito on a
white wall). The authoritative per-ad-group mapping is `data.csv` inside
`../ad_assets_export_with_images.zip`; see `../README.md` for the image map.
